Cinnamon Roll

Description

A delicious cinnamon roll made with a flaky, buttery dough and a sweet cinnamon-sugar filling.

Ingredients

  • 1 cup warm milk
  • 2 1/4 teaspoons active dry yeast
  • 1/2 cup sugar
  • 1 egg
  • 1/2 cup butter, melted
  • 3 cups all-purpose flour
  • 1 teaspoon salt
  • 1/2 cup brown sugar
  • 2 tablespoons cinnamon

Steps

  1. In a small bowl, dissolve yeast in warm milk and let it sit for 5 minutes.
  2. In a large bowl, combine sugar, egg, melted butter, and salt. Add the yeast mixture and mix well.
  3. Gradually add flour and knead the dough until smooth and elastic. Cover and let it rise for 1 hour.
  4. Roll out the dough into a rectangle. Spread the brown sugar and cinnamon mixture over the dough.
  5. Roll up the dough tightly and cut into slices. Place the slices in a greased baking dish.
  6. Cover and let them rise for another 30 minutes. Preheat the oven to 350°F (175°C).
  7. Bake for 25-30 minutes or until golden brown. Let them cool before serving.
