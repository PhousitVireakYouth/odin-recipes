Pie

Description

A delicious pie made with a flaky crust and a sweet filling.

Ingredients

  • 2 cups all-purpose flour
  • 1/2 cup unsalted butter, chilled and cubed
  • 1/4 cup ice water
  • 1/4 teaspoon salt
  • 1/2 cup sugar
  • 2 cups fruit filling (such as apple, cherry, or blueberry)

Steps

  1. Preheat your oven to 425°F (220°C).
  2. Roll out the chilled pie crust and place it in a 9-inch pie dish.
  3. In a large bowl, combine the fruit filling and sugar. Mix well.
  4. Pour the filling into the pie crust.
  5. Roll out the second pie crust and place it over the filling.
  6. Crimp the edges of the crusts together to seal.
  7. Cut several slits in the top crust to allow steam to escape.
  8. Bake for 15 minutes, then reduce the heat to 350°F (175°C) and continue baking for another 35-45 minutes, or until the crust is golden brown and the filling is bubbly.
  9. Allow the pie to cool completely before serving.
